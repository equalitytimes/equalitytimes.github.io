Write for Equality Times.

Share original legal writing, research and public-education work that contributes to informed understanding of law and society.

Who Can Submit

Thoughtful voices, carefully edited.

We welcome original submissions from law students, researchers, educators, practitioners and informed writers whose work aligns with the platform's areas of interest.

Topics

Law, constitutional issues, equality, human rights, criminal justice, technology, AI, cyber law, digital rights and related questions.

What We Look For

Originality, clarity, sound reasoning, responsible sourcing and a clear contribution to public legal understanding.

Editorial Review

Every submission is reviewed before publication. Equality Times may edit for clarity, structure, accuracy and style.

Original Work

Submit work that you have the right to publish and that is not knowingly plagiarised or misleading.
